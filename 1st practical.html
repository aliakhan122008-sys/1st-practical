Easy Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, beat the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until just combined. Don't overmix!
  4. Heat a lightly oiled frying pan or griddle over medium-high heat.
  5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface, then flip and cook until golden brown.
  7. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
